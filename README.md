# Better-Vanilla-Experience-
🌟 Better Vanilla Exp. (1.21.11 Fabric) Bored of vanilla? This Vanilla+ pack hardens survival, keeping the classic vibe. Features: custom world gen, seasons, harsh winters, historical weapons, 3D depth, and top optimization. Try it!
Better Vanilla Experience — Mod Guide (Version 1.0)
Below is a quick guide to the main mods included in the build, so you know what features await you in the game!

============================================================================
🌍 WORLD EXPLORATION AND GENERATION
=======================================================================================
• Promenade / Blooming Biosphere / Ecologics - completely overhaul world generation, adding new picturesque biomes, trees, and plants.
• Dungeons and Taverns / Explorify / Grim Kingdoms / Structory — fills the world with a multitude of detailed structures: abandoned taverns, towers, fortresses, underground dungeons, and new types of villages.
• Sparse Structures — balances the spawning of custom dungeons so they don't overlap and the world appears harmonious.
• Better Archeology — expands the archeology mechanics, adding new fossils, artifacts, and hidden structures for excavation.
• Better Village — completely reworks and decorates vanilla villages, making them unique to each biome.

===========================================================================
🐾 ANIMAL WORLD (FAUNA)
====================================================================================
• AnimalGarden Series (Common Raven, Crocodile, Lion, Hippopotamus, Snow Leopard, Sea Otter, etc.) — a massive set of mods that adds dozens of real-life animal species to all biomes: from ravens and otters to lions, hippos, and crocodiles.
• Cube Animals — replaces or supplements the models of some animals, making them more detailed and fitting into the game's style.
• Jagmkiwis — adds kiwi birds with their own mechanics and behavior.

===========================================================================
❄️ SEASONS, CLIMATE, AND SURVIVAL
=================================================================================
• Serene Seasons — a fully dynamic change of seasons (spring, summer, fall, winter). Changes foliage color, weather, and blocks the growth of regular farmland in winter.
• Frostful / Scorchful / Thermoo — add advanced temperature, freezing, and overheating mechanics. In winter, you need to dress warmly and warm yourself by a fire, and in summer, seek shade or cool places.
• Climate Rivers — adjusts the behavior of rivers and bodies of water to the current climate and biome.
• CookYourFood / Cooking Tips themselves — encourages a more thoughtful approach to cooking, making food an important part of winter survival.

==========================================================================
⚔️ COMBAT, WEAPONS, AND MOBS
==================================================================================
• Weapons of Rome / Weapons of Japan / Weapons of India — huge packs of historical melee weapons with their own mechanics and unique appearance.
• Musket Mod Reloaded — adds flintlock muskets and pistols for effective long-range combat.
• Guard Villagers — Villagers can now become armed guards who patrol the area and protect homes from monsters.
• Illager Invasion — a major improvement to bandits: new enemy types, advanced AI, and brutal raids on villages.
• Eldritch Mobs / Better Vanilla Mobs / Mutant Zombies — increase the difficulty of monsters by adding custom abilities, rare spawns of elite enemies, and bosses (such as Mutant Zombies), making nighttime raids truly dangerous.
• World Events — triggers random events in the world, creating unexpected situations for the player.

============================================================================
🎭 ATMOSPHERE, ANIMATIONS, AND VISUALS
===================================================================================
• Sound Physics Remastered — Cool sound physics, adding realistic echoes in caves and muffling sounds behind block walls.
• SkinLayers3D — Gives player skins volume by transforming the flat pixels of the second layer of clothing into realistic 3D models. • Not Enough Animations — adds a bunch of new third-person animations to your character (swimming, eating, climbing, holding objects).
• Falling Leaves — adds a beautiful effect of falling leaves from trees, depending on the season.
• Particle Rain / ParticleEffects / Visuality — replaces the standard rain with cool drops, adds fog during storms, and adds juicy, fine particles when hitting mobs.
• Blur — beautifully blurs the background when opening the inventory or menu.

=============================================================================
🎒 INTERFACE, STORAGE AND CONVENIENCE
====================================================================================
• Xaero's WorldMap / Waypoints — a fully-fledged interactive world map where you can place waypoints to ensure you never lose your home or dungeon.
• JEI (Just Enough Items) — an essential mod that shows all the crafting recipes and methods for obtaining any item right in the game.
• Comforts — adds sleeping bags that allow you to skip a night on a hike without resetting your spawn point at your home bed.
